Validated Input Form

Centralized Directory
Both Roles

Interactive roster displaying student profiles, department badges, real-time calculated attendance rate (%), average marks, and overall grade status.

3. Update Student Details

Live In-Place Editing
Academic Coordinator

Modify student info, contact phone, program stream, academic standing, or enrollment status anytime through intuitive edit modals.

4. Delete Student Records

Safe Cascade Deletion

Safely remove outdated or departed student profiles and their associated academic marks and attendance history with confirmation dialogs.

Instant Fast Query

Automated Analytics
